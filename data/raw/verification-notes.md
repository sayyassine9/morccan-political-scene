## Leadership 2025–2026
- **RNI**: Aziz Akhannouch announced he would not seek a third term or stand in the 2026 legislative election; Mohamed Chaouki (MP for Boulemane since 2021, RNI group chair since April 2023, ex-chair of the Finance committee) was elected president at an extraordinary congress in El Jadida on 7 Feb 2026 with 1,910 of 1,933 valid votes (Médias24, Le Desk, Le360, APA, Morocco World News). Akhannouch remains head of government until the election.
- **USFP**: Driss Lachgar re-elected 17 Oct 2025 for a fourth term despite internal opposition (Maroc Hebdo, Libération).
- **PJD**: Abdelilah Benkirane re-elected at Bouznika in April 2025 for a mandate to 2029 (Médias24 "qui tient les partis", Jan 2026).

## 2025–2026 events
- **GenZ 212**: protests from late Sept 2025 (Discord-born collective, 200k+ members), demands on health/education/jobs, "hospitals not stadiums"; call for government resignation 2–3 Oct 2025; royal speech at Parliament 10 Oct 2025 on social justice and territorial inequality without naming the movement; government measures 19 Oct 2025 (Nadia Fettah: 140 bn MAD for health and education in 2026 budget, +18%; 27,000 posts; direct social aid extended to 4 M households); protests continued 25–26 Oct (France 24, Médias24, allAfrica, Agence Ecofin).
- **"Fraqchia" inquiry (2026)**: opposition demand for a parliamentary inquiry into livestock/red-meat import subsidies (opposition estimate 13 bn MAD vs majority 464 M MAD). PAM and Istiqlal initially joined the opposition, isolating RNI; initiative failed on procedural divisions (Le360, TelQuel 30 Jun 2026).
- **2026 election context**: Sunergia poll on voting intentions split (42.3% positive / 38.6% negative / 19.1% undecided); Polymarket prediction market favoured PAM (not a poll). Most analysts expect the RNI–PAM–Istiqlal majority to be renewed with adjustments (Mondafrique, Express TV, industries.ma).

## Party finance
- Cour des comptes 2023 report: state support actually granted 60.38 M MAD to 17 parties (43% of 140 M MAD credits), –25.5% vs 2022 (81.17 M MAD); largest declared resources RNI 38.69 M, Istiqlal 17.80 M, PAM 14.67 M; USFP alone received women's-representation support (100,813.20 MAD); 15 parties owe 21.96 M MAD (ecoactu, LesEco, Yabiladi, Le360).
- 2022: 29 of 34 parties filed accounts; 153 M MAD declared, 53% from the state. Unreturned unjustified support at end-2022: RNI 13,377,681.18 MAD; PJD 5,785,122.26 MAD; PAM and USFP 0 (Cour des comptes synthesis via Le360/Médias24).
- 2021: total state support 407.09 M MAD (85.56% campaign contribution, 14.39% annual management support, 0.05% women's representation); parties' total declared resources 499.69 M MAD.
- Le360 "Quel parti politique coûte le plus à l'État?" (annual management support, year not stated in the excerpt): PAM 12.6 M, Istiqlal 6.32 M, RNI 5.4 M, MP 4.11 M, USFP 3.47 M MAD; RNI 13% state-dependent vs PAM 67%. Recorded with low confidence because the fiscal year was not confirmed.
